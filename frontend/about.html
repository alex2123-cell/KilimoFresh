About us

A cooperative network, not a middleman

KilimoFresh is the shared sourcing desk of smallholder cooperatives in Machakos County. It exists so that farmers know their buyer before they harvest, and buyers know their supply before they plan a menu.

The problem we are solving

Around Machakos, Athi River, Kangundo, Matuu and Mwala, peri-urban farmers often harvest without a confirmed buyer. Prices swing from week to week, produce that is not sold within days spoils, and brokers set the farm-gate price.

Institutional buyers have the opposite problem. A school bursar or hotel storekeeper cannot easily see what is available nearby, in what grade, at what price, or when.

KilimoFresh puts both sides on one page: a catalog of what is being harvested, a clear price, and a direct way to request it.

How the network runs

  • Each cooperative reports its expected harvest to the desk every week.
  • A field officer grades the produce at the collection point.
  • The admin publishes the listing with a short code such as TOM-G1-ATH (Grade 1 tomatoes, Athi River).
  • Buyers send a requisition; the desk confirms supply by phone and arranges delivery.

Fair pricing

The price on each card is the price the buyer pays per unit. The cooperative keeps a fixed service share to cover grading, crates and the desk; the rest is the farmer’s farm-gate price.

Delivery is charged separately, per zone, and shown before you order.
