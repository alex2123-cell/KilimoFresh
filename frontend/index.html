How it works

From catalog to confirmed order

  1. Find the produce

    Search the catalog and filter by category, farm location, price and stock. Every card shows grade, price, minimum order and harvest date.

  2. Estimate the cost

    Enter a quantity and a delivery zone. You see quantity × unit price plus the delivery fee, and a warning if you are below the minimum order.

  3. Send a requisition

    Submit the form and get a reference number with a digitally signed receipt. The cooperative desk calls you to confirm, or you follow up on WhatsApp.
