# Product and page photos

Put your own photos here (WebP or JPG, about 800 px wide, under 150 KB each).

To show a photo on a catalog card, add an `image` field to that listing in
`backend/data/produce.json`, or fill "Photo file" in the admin catalog form:

    "image": "tomatoes.webp"

To use a photo anywhere in a page:

    <img src="assets/images/farm-hero.webp" alt="Farmers sorting tomatoes in Athi River"
         width="800" height="533" loading="lazy">

Only use photos you took yourself or that are free to reuse, and credit them.
