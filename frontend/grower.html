Farming advisories

Getting more of your harvest to market

Harvest in the cool hours

Pick early in the morning and keep crates in the shade. Produce that starts cool stays fresh for longer on the road.

Sort at the farm

Separate Grade 1 from Grade 2 before the collection point. One damaged fruit in a crate lowers the grade of the whole crate.

Use ventilated crates

Plastic crates stacked no more than five high bruise less than sacks, and can be washed and reused.

Report the harvest date early

Tell the desk a week before you harvest. Listings that go up early are usually requested before picking.

Stagger your planting

Planting a section every two weeks spreads the harvest, so you supply a buyer every week instead of once.

Keep simple records

Write down planting dates, inputs and yields per plot. Buyers increasingly ask where and how produce was grown.
